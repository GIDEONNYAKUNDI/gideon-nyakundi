Biography

Dr. Gideon Moseti Nyakundi

DHAMHAENGCHTLACHE

The Perfect Gift for Nyamira

Healthcare executive, clinical engineer and emergency management leader, with over two decades of leadership in some of the most respected health systems in the United States and across the world.

A son of Nyamira

Dr. Gideon Moseti Nyakundi was born in Manga and raised in the villages of Nyamira County. From those humble beginnings he rose to the executive level of world healthcare, carrying with him the values of his community: hard work, faith, respect for elders and service to others.

A career built on patient safety

Dr. Nyakundi began his career as a Clinical Engineer at the Mayo Clinic, supporting the advanced medical technology behind world class patient care. He went on to serve as an open heart surgery technologist at Hennepin County Medical Center (HCMC), working at the bedside in one of the most demanding fields of medicine.

Alongside this work, he partnered with a number of companies to review life support medical equipment in forensic studies and in investigations of sentinel events: the serious, unexpected incidents that shape patient safety standards. His reviews covered pacemakers, ventilators, brain surgery equipment and anaesthesia systems.

Leading large health systems

As Senior Director at Denver Health, he oversaw clinical technology across more than 60 facilities, including the city's ambulance system. He then joined the University of Maryland Medical System as an executive leader in healthcare services, and worked with Johns Hopkins as a process improvement consultant, helping to make care safer and more efficient.

Emergency management and community resilience

Dr. Nyakundi was later promoted to serve as Director of Emergency Management in the Permian Basin of West Texas. There he works hand in hand with hospitals, emergency medical services, fire departments, police and schools to prepare for and manage emergencies and to build community resilience, while also overseeing the biomedical engineering division.

Coming home to serve

Today, Dr. Nyakundi brings this world class experience home. With proven leadership in health systems, patient safety and crisis response, he stands ready to serve the people of Nyamira: the perfect gift for Nyamira.
