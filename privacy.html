Privacy notice

Dr. Gideon Moseti Nyakundi campaign for Governor, Nyamira County · Last updated 31 October 2026

In short: we collect only what we need to organise the campaign, we keep it on our own secure server, only two authorised campaign officials can see it, and we never sell it. You can ask us to see, correct or delete your details at any time.

Who we are

This website is run by the campaign of Dr. Gideon Moseti Nyakundi for Governor of Nyamira County. For the purposes of Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, the campaign is the data controller for the information you give us here.

What we collect

  • Volunteers: your name, phone number, constituency and ward, and anything else you choose to add (village, age group, how you would like to help, your priority, e-mail).
  • Polling station agents: your name, phone number, ward, polling station and stream, whether you are registered there, past experience and training availability, and optionally your e-mail.
  • Voter registration pledges: your name, phone number, constituency, ward and where you are with registration.
  • Pledges: your name, phone number, the amount and how you would like to give. We never ask for card or bank details on this website.

We do not ask for your national ID number on this website. Polling agent accreditation documents are collected in person at training.

Why we use it

  • To call or message you about the campaign, volunteering, agent training and events.
  • To organise polling station agents so every stream is covered on election day.
  • To help you register to vote if you ask for help.
  • To follow up pledges and issue receipts through official campaign channels.

We use your details only because you agreed to it when you ticked the consent box. You can withdraw that consent at any time.

Who can see it

Your details are stored in a password-protected database on the campaign's own hosting account. Only authorised campaign officials can sign in to see them. We do not sell, rent or share your details with marketers or anyone outside the campaign. We may send you SMS or e-mail through a messaging provider acting only on our instructions.

How long we keep it

We keep your details for the 2027 election campaign and delete them within twelve months after the election, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you have the right to:

  • be told how your information is used (this notice);
  • see the information we hold about you;
  • have it corrected if it is wrong;
  • have it deleted, and to object to us using it;
  • stop campaign messages: reply STOP to any SMS, or tell us.

To use any of these rights, call, SMS or WhatsApp the campaign on 0723 636 511. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (odpc.go.ke).

Security

The website uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS). The campaign database cannot be reached from the internet directly, sign-in is limited and protected against repeated guessing, and the forms are protected against spam and automated abuse.
