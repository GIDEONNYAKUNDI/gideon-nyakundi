A scholar-practitioner

Dr. Nyakundi's scholarship is practical by design. Each book and study begins with a problem he has met in his own work, whether an infection carried on a surface that was never cleaned, a health insurance system that registers people it cannot pay for, or a school system in the middle of reform. It then sets out the evidence and a working answer. His research has been carried out at the bedside, in forensic investigations of medical equipment, in the evaluation of national health systems and in the villages of Nyamira.

Book 1 · Healthcare quality and patient safety

Hospital Infection Control Guidelines

Hospital Environmental Cleaning and Disinfection Process Framework

Paperback

On any given day, about one in 38 hospital patients has an infection acquired during care, and many of those infections travel on surfaces and equipment that were missed, rushed or never documented. This guide gives hospitals a complete, field-tested system for closing those gaps.

Research approach. Grew out of direct observation of cleaning practice in an acute care hospital and its rural health clinic; the author's own photographs illustrate real findings. Every chapter cites the CMS and Joint Commission requirements that apply, aligned with CDC, EPA, OSHA and ICMR guidance.

  • Standard and isolation precautions; room, operating room and special area cleaning
  • Medical equipment cleaning programs and clean status tagging; high level disinfection and sterilization
  • Water, air and construction safety; monitoring, surveillance and survey readiness
  • Two chapters on Ebola and other highly contagious diseases, drawing on CDC's 2026 guidance
  • Appendices of ready-to-use checklists, logs, matrices and templates

Book 2 · Health policy and health financing

Healing Kenya's Healthcare System: Kenya Healthcare-360

Affordable, Accessible, Quality

Paperback

A reform plan for Kenya's overstretched, underfunded and unevenly distributed health sector. Its premise: Kenya need not abandon the goal of universal health coverage that the Social Health Authority was built to achieve, but it needs one coherent system in which registration, contribution and coverage are designed together.

Research approach. The diagnosis is built entirely on public record: National Assembly committee findings, High Court rulings, statements by SHA's own chief executive and the Cabinet Secretary for Health, provider association data and established Kenyan news reporting, each attributed and dated so it can be verified. The reform framework draws on international comparative evidence chosen for relevance to Kenya.

  • Closes the gaps between who is registered and who contributes, and between what providers are owed and what they are paid
  • Each proposal names the documented SHA failure it corrects and the comparative example that informed it
  • Follows care from the village dispensary to the national referral hospital

Book 3 · Education policy

The Kenya Education System Reforms

Re-design of the Education System, from Early Childhood to University

Paperback

Asks how Kenya's school system, from Early Childhood Development through university, can be redesigned to deliver quality, equitable and sustainable learning for every child in every county, regardless of household income.

Research approach. Written for policymakers and county education officials, researchers and graduate students, and the headteachers, teachers, parents and university administrators living the transition. Follows the arc of a learner's life and uses comparative case studies from countries that have made similar reforms.

  • Evidence-based account of the shift from 8-4-4 to the 2-6-3-3-3 Competency-Based Curriculum and Competency-Based Education
  • The higher education funding crisis and what has been tried before
  • A policy framework built on access, curriculum integrity, teacher capacity, infrastructure, financing, TVET integration, governance and resilience

Book 4 · Global health policy

Global Healthcare Policy

Paperback

A master's and doctoral level text on health policy across nations, written for graduate students and researchers and for government policymakers around the world. It examines how health systems are organised, financed and governed, and what countries can learn from one another.

Research approach. Draws on the author's evaluation of health systems in the United States and his consulting work in more than ten countries.

  • Written for graduate study at master's and doctoral level
  • A reference for government policymakers in Kenya and globally

Book 5 · Healthcare leadership

Authentic Empathy Manual for Healthcare Leaders

Transforming Patient Care Through Purpose-Driven Leadership and Compassionate Practice

Paperback

A training manual for healthcare leaders on authentic empathy, resilience and service, with principles that apply to leaders in any organization and to anyone seeking personal growth.

Research approach. Grounded in real experience across diverse healthcare settings, from community outreach and frontline clinical practice to leadership in high-stakes environments, together with global healthcare advocacy and system improvement.

Book 6 · Cultural history

Where the Hills Remember: Gusii People

One Gusii Family, a People, and a Changing World

Paperback

Neither pure history nor pure memoir: an honest, carefully drawn portrait of how the Gusii people's oldest patterns of kinship, land, belief and obligation met the twentieth century, and what survived the meeting.

Research approach. Records the particular details of one family's life in the Gusii Highlands: the homestead and its division of labor, the elders who settled disputes by firelight, and the shift from the rhythms of a rain-fed year to those of school terms and wage labor. Written for readers both inside and outside the community.

Book 7 · African culture and development

The Seed of the Lost Nation

Chronicles of Culture and Traditions

Paperback, 132 pages, published 22 September 2024

Through his own story, the author describes African cultural practices that are now lost, how colonialism helped derail them, and the problems that hold Africa back, including leaders who shut out the continent's own well-educated experts.

Research approach. Argues that education, sensitization and awareness are pivotal if Africa is to be transformed, and asks where ordinary citizens (wananchi) can find a way forward.

Research and applied scholarship

Alongside the books, Dr. Nyakundi's research has taken the form of investigations, evaluations and field studies carried out within health systems.

Doctoral research: medication and patient safety

His doctoral thesis studied the safety of medicines and of patients in medical-surgical (med-surg) and intensive care units (ICU), where patients are most vulnerable to errors in medication and care.

Forensic review of life-support technology

Partnered with a number of companies to review life-support medical equipment in forensic studies and in investigations of sentinel events, the serious unexpected incidents that shape patient safety standards: pacemakers, ventilators, brain surgery equipment and anaesthesia systems.

Health system evaluation

Took a key part in evaluating the Affordable Care Act in the United States, and has evaluated health systems across the world, serving as a consultant in more than ten countries.

Process improvement in large health systems

Process improvement consultant with Johns Hopkins while an executive leader in healthcare services at the University of Maryland Medical System; oversaw clinical technology across more than 60 facilities, including the city ambulance system, at Denver Health.

Emergency preparedness and community resilience

As Director of Emergency Management in the Permian Basin of West Texas, leads hazard vulnerability analysis and emergency planning with hospitals, ambulance services, fire, police and schools, and designed and ran a multi-agency mass casualty exercise and its after-action review.

Infection prevention field research

Direct observation of environmental cleaning and disinfection in an acute care hospital and a rural health clinic, documented with original photographs, which became Hospital Infection Control Guidelines.

Public policy research from the public record

Every figure and claim in Kenya Healthcare-360 is attributed to a parliamentary finding, court ruling, official statement or published report so readers can verify it; the education book applies comparative case studies from countries that made similar reforms.

Cultural history and oral memory

Two books record Gusii and wider African culture: family memory, elders' practice, proverbs and the traditions that modernization has displaced, written so that the knowledge is not lost.
